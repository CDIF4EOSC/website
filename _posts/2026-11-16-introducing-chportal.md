[CHPortal](https://chportal.culturalheritagecloud.eu/) is now online! Developed within the ECHOES project, it is a semantic artefact catalogue dedicated to cultural heritage, contributing to the European Collaborative Cloud for Cultural Heritage.

Built on OntoPortal, CHPortal allows users to publish, browse and reuse ontologies, thesauri and controlled vocabularies. It aims to help cultural heritage professionals and researchers discover semantic resources and use them to describe and connect their data.

CHPortal was recently presented at the SWODCH 2026 workshop in Bari, Italy, where it received positive feedback from participants. We will continue gathering feedback from the cultural heritage community as we develop the catalogue and its documentation.

We invite researchers, cultural heritage institutions and vocabulary providers to explore the portal and contribute their resources.
